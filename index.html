How to play

Find the hidden equation that equals the target. Every guess must be a valid equation of the right length that also equals the target — a guess that doesn't hit the target isn't accepted and doesn't cost a turn.

Standard order of operations: × and ÷ before + and −, then left to right. Numbers can't start with 0.

Structure hint. The badge beside each guess tells you whether your equation has the same shape as the hidden one — the same pattern of numbers and operators, like DD·o·D·o·D. A ✓ means only the symbols are wrong, not their arrangement.

If your guess uses exactly the hidden equation's symbols in a different order and still equals the target, it counts as a win.

Easy: 5 slots, one operator · Medium: 6 slots, two operators · Hard: 8 slots, two or three operators. The Daily puzzle is the same for everyone on a given day; Random gives a fresh one any time.
